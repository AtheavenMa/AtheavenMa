# Haotian Ma

Ph.D. Candidate · **UW–Madison CS** · Founder & Maintainer, **[OpenSAE](https://github.com/OpenSAE)**

[Website](https://atheavenma.github.io/) · [CV](https://atheavenma.github.io/CV.pdf) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/Atheaven-Ma) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Hunter26666666) · [Email](mailto:hma232@wisc.edu)

I study how learned representations shape model behavior and when they support faithful explanations. My research spans mechanistic interpretability, LLM interventions, and AI for scientific discovery.

My featured work includes [*Spectral-Spatial Interpretation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21114) (**NeurIPS 2026**), [*SegJointGene*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.08.698474v1.full.pdf) (**Bioinformatics 2026**), and [*Quantification and Analysis of Layer-wise and Pixel-wise Information Discarding*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/ma22b.html) (**ICML 2022 Spotlight**).

At [OpenSAE](https://github.com/OpenSAE), I develop open-source research infrastructure for understanding and evaluating sparse autoencoder features, testing explanations, and performing controlled interventions in language models.

<details>
<summary>Research directions</summary>

- **Faithful representations and mechanistic interpretability.** Understand learned features and test whether they support faithful explanations and interventions.
- **Interpretable AI for scientific discovery.** Connect model predictions to spatial, spectral, and molecular structures.
- **Compositionality and systematic reasoning.** Study how model components and structural transformations combine and generalize.
- **Reliable language models and AI safety.** Examine changing preferences, underspecified user identities, and risks of private-information leakage.

</details>
