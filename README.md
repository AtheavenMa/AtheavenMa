My goal is to explain AI behavior from the bottom up, starting with learned features and building toward abstract concepts. To pursue this goal, I develop methods to understand how learned representations shape model behavior across interventions, combinations, and changing contexts. My research combines theory, method development, controlled interventions, and large-scale evaluation across mechanistic interpretability, LLM reasoning, AI safety, and AI for scientific discovery.

## Research

- **Faithful Representations and Mechanistic Interpretability** — Understand feature representations and how those representations support faithful explanations and interventions.
- **Interpretable AI for Scientific Discovery** — Develop interpretable methods that connect model decisions to scientifically meaningful spatial, spectral, and molecular structure.
- **Compositionality, Intervention, and Systematic Reasoning in LLMs** — Study when model components and structural transformations can be combined, reused, or generalized reliably in unseen settings.
- **Reliable LLMs and AI Safety** — Characterize how AI systems remain reliable under uncertain preferences, underspecified user identities, and risks of private information leakage.

## Open-source

- 🧱 [**SAEBase**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAEBase)

    Research infrastructure for understanding, testing, and modifying the internal features of AI models.

- 🧩 [**SAR — Behavioral Compositionality of Sparse Autoencoder Representations**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/SAE-Behavioral-Compositionality)

    Measures how much model behavior depends on interactions between its learned features.

- 🔀 [**RSS — Representation Redistribution in Sparse Autoencoders**](https://github.com/OpenSAE/Redistribution-Sensitivity-Score)

    Detects consistent shifts in SAE feature activity when the same request is presented differently.

- 🔥 [**SSI — Spectral-Spatial Interpretation**](https://github.com/AtheavenMa/Spectral-Spatial-Interpretation)

    Explains where models find evidence and which channel matter at each location.

- 🎯 [**SymSelect — Learning Which Transformations to Trust for Systematic Reasoning**](https://github.com/AtheavenMa/SymSelect)

    Helps models learn from altered examples without knowing which alterations preserve correct answers.

- ⏳ [**The Horizon of Reward Guidance**](https://github.com/AtheavenMa/The-Horizon-of-Reward-Guidance)

    Explains how long human feedback can reliably guide model as its behavior changes.
